Biography

Worth its
weight in soul

AMXXR — also known as Peace Beloved — is an MC from Mount Vernon, New York: the same square mile of soil that gave hip-hop Heavy D and Pete Rock. That lineage isn't a coincidence. It's the inheritance.

In 2021, AMXXR became the first artist signed to Pete Rock's Tru Soul Records, announcing the partnership with the mixtape 21 Grams: Worth It's Weight In Soul — every beat by the Soul Brother #1, every bar by AMXXR. The full-length Dope Boy Soul followed the same year, and critics pointed to the distinct flow, clever wordplay and thought-provoking storytelling that earned him the seat.

Beloved In The Sky (2022) deepened the Peace Beloved alias — grief, faith and elevation over heavyweight soul. THE CITY NEVER SLEEPS BELOVED (2024) is the most complete statement yet: the city after midnight, told by someone who never left it.

The mission is simple: keep soul music's bloodline alive in modern hip-hop, build directly with the fans who carry it, and make every record worth its weight.
